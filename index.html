Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Google review links, QR codes, and NFC contactless cards

How do I create a direct 5-star Google review link?

Simply search for your business name using our Find Business tool or copy your Google Maps share link and paste it here. Our system resolves your Google Place ID and crafts a direct URL that immediately opens the 5-star review submission pop-up for your customers.

How does writing to NFC review cards work?

Download our free Android APK. After generating your review URL, tap 'Write to NFC Tag', then hold your NFC card, coin, or sticker to the back of your phone. The app formats the tag and writes your review link in under a second with zero subscription fees.

Can I print the generated QR code for my business?

Yes! The generated QR code is high-resolution and downloadable in PNG format. You can print it on table talkers, restaurant menus, cash register counters, business cards, and packaging stickers.

Is ReviewLinkGenerator free and does it require an API key?

Yes, it is 100% free and open-source. It does not require any Google Cloud API key, credit card, or user registration. All Place ID resolution and link formatting runs directly in your browser or Android device.
