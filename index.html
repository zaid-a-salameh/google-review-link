Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Google review links, QR codes, and NFC contactless cards

How do I create a direct 5-star Google review link?

Search for your business name using the Find Business tab or paste your Google Maps link. The tool resolves your Place ID and crafts a direct URL that automatically prompts customers for a 5-star review.

What NFC tags and cards are supported?

Quick NFC supports all standard NTAG chips (NTAG213, NTAG215, NTAG216) as well as compatible NFC stickers, coins, keyfobs, and PVC cards available on the market.

How does Wi-Fi NFC card setup and cloning work?

Enter your Wi-Fi name and password or hold an existing Wi-Fi NFC card to read it. Touch a writable NFC card to write the configuration so guests can connect instantly without typing passwords.

Can I protect my cards from unauthorized modification?

Yes, you can set a 4-digit PIN password on supported NTAG chips to protect cards from being overwritten by strangers, or permanently lock them to Read-Only mode for public displays.

Can I generate and download high-resolution QR codes?

Yes! Click the QR Code button to instantly create a clean, high-resolution PNG image suitable for printing on table tents, counter stickers, packaging, and business cards.

Is Quick NFC completely free and private?

Yes, it is 100% free and open-source under the MIT license. It requires no user account, no subscription, and no Google Cloud API key, and it works completely offline.
